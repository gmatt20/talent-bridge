## Overview

We are building an **AI-powered community collaboration platform** for OCC Hacks 2026, inspired by the mission of the [Community Innovations Foundation (CIF)](https://www.cifdn.org/).

Our platform helps Orange County businesses, nonprofits, students, and professional mentors discover opportunities, connect, and collaborate on real-world technology projects.

## Our Story

One of our team members is a STEAM instructor at **Dreams for Schools**, where students learn technology through hands-on education.

At a recent networking event, they met the board members of Community Innovations Foundation and discovered mutual connections between CIF and Dreams for Schools.

Inspired by CIF's mission and its existing **Innovators Lab**, we wanted to explore how technology could make community-driven collaboration even more accessible and seamless.

## The Problem

CIF's Innovators Lab already connects students, mentors, and community partners to build meaningful technological solutions.

**The opportunity isn't to create these connections from scratch, but to make the existing process easier, faster, and more accessible.**

Students, businesses, and mentors have different backgrounds, technical capabilities, and needs. Navigating project discovery, communicating requirements, identifying suitable collaborators, and coordinating participation can introduce unnecessary friction.

We want to reduce these barriers through a unified, intuitive platform.

## Our Solution

A three-sided platform supporting the existing community innovation ecosystem.

### Businesses & Nonprofits

- Create organization profiles and submit project challenges.
- Describe business requirements in accessible language.
- Discover and collaborate with interested students and mentors.

### Students

- Showcase skills, interests, and learning goals.
- Discover real-world projects from local organizations.
- Receive AI-powered project recommendations.
- Collaborate with organizations and mentors.

### Mentors

- Showcase professional expertise and availability.
- Discover projects aligned with their experience.
- Guide student teams through practical challenges.

## How AI Helps

AI acts as an intelligent facilitator, not a replacement for human collaboration.

- **Requirements analysis:** Translate business needs into structured project briefs.
- **Student matchmaking:** Recommend relevant projects based on skills, interests, and learning goals.
- **Mentor matchmaking:** Connect teams with relevant professional expertise.
- **Explainable recommendations:** Help participants understand why opportunities are suitable.

## Our Mission

**Simplify and strengthen the collaboration between community organizations, students, and mentors through accessible technology and AI.**

We aim to extend the reach of initiatives like CIF's Innovators Lab by removing technological barriers, streamlining coordination, and helping more students participate in meaningful community projects.

## Hackathon Goal

Demonstrate one complete user journey:

**Business submits a challenge → AI structures requirements → Student discovers a matching project → Mentor joins → Team collaboration begins.**

The platform should feel approachable, intuitive, and useful to people regardless of their technical experience.

## Guiding Principles

- **Complement, don't replace:** Build upon CIF's existing programs and community relationships.
- **Accessibility first:** Make the experience easy for technical and nontechnical users.
- **Community impact:** Focus on meaningful, real-world challenges.
- **Learning by doing:** Give students practical, mentor-supported experience.
- **Human-centered AI:** AI recommends and assists; people make decisions.
- **Simplicity:** Remove unnecessary steps from collaboration.

## Our Vision

**CIF already brings the community together. We're building technology that makes working together easier.**
